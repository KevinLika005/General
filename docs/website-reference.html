01 Purpose & goal

General Trading is a B2B technical equipment catalog for construction machinery, tools, materials, transport assets, and site-support products, operated under the parent company GENERAL and based in Tirana, Albania.

The site's job is not to sell online. It exists to let procurement teams, contractors, and fleet operators browse a serious technical catalog, build up a request, and hand it to the sales team as one structured inquiry. Everything after that — pricing negotiation, inspection, contracts, delivery — is handled offline, company to company.

Working definition

An inquiry-commerce catalog: full ecommerce browsing and filtering mechanics (search, categories, brands, product detail, a cart-like list), with no checkout, no online payment, and no live pricing engine at the end of it.

02 Business model

The commercial flow is deliberately offline past the point of first contact:

  1. A visitor browses the catalog by category, brand, model, SKU, or keyword.
  2. They add one or more products to an Inquiry List (kept in the browser, not a server cart).
  3. They submit one Request Quote form covering the whole list, or a general Contact form for a non-product question.
  4. The sales team receives the request by email and takes it from there: pricing, technical clarification, inspection scheduling, delivery/logistics, and contract terms all happen directly between the two companies.

Three pricing modes exist per product, reflecting that this is quote-driven, not shelf-priced, retail:

ModeShown asMeaning
visibleEUR 78,500Firm listed price
starting-fromStarting from EUR 89,500Floor price, variant-dependent
price-on-requestPrice on requestNo price shown; inquiry required

Products also carry an availability state — available, reserved, incoming, or sold — which controls whether an item can still be added to the inquiry list.

03 Technical stack

Vite 5 React 18 + TypeScript React Router 7 Tailwind CSS 3 i18next / react-i18next lucide-react PHP mail endpoint

The frontend is a single-page app: all catalog, brand, and content data lives in static TypeScript files under src/data rather than a database or CMS. There is no backend for the catalog itself — the only server-side piece is a small PHP script (server/mail/send.php) that receives form submissions and emails them to the sales inbox.

This means updating the catalog today means editing code (guided by INVENTORY_GUIDE.md at the repo root), not logging into an admin panel. That trade-off is worth flagging early: it keeps the stack simple while the catalog is small, but it will not scale gracefully once someone other than a developer needs to manage inventory day to day.

07 Inquiry & email pipeline

Two forms carry every lead:

  • Contact form (/contact) — general inquiries: name, company, contact details, inquiry type, timeline, message.
  • Request Quote form (/request-quote) — everything the contact form has, plus company role, VAT number, delivery preference, and the full product list pulled from the Inquiry List (quantities, notes, pricing, SKUs, product URLs).

Submissions post to a PHP endpoint (server/mail/send.php) which validates the payload, checks a honeypot field and submission timing for spam, then sends the email via SMTP with the visitor's address set only as Reply-To — never as the sending address. Local development uses Mailpit/MailHog as an SMTP catcher; production needs real SMTP credentials configured in a git-ignored server/mail/config.local.php, which must never be committed. Full setup and test steps are documented in docs/email-system.md.

08 Localization

The site is bilingual, Albanian and English, via i18next. Albanian (sq) is the default and fallback language; the visitor's choice is remembered in localStorage. Locale strings live in src/i18n/locales/en.ts and sq.ts, with a separate catalogLocale.ts helper that overlays translated copy onto the base English data in src/data. An npm run audit:i18n script checks for missing keys or English text leaking into the Albanian experience.

09 Current status

Two internal audits already sit in the repo root and are worth reading in full before major work: PROJECT_COMPLETION_TODOS.md (a launch-readiness checklist) and redesign-plan.md (a visual/structural redesign brief toward a lighter, Rafin navy/gold industrial-marketplace look). Their headline findings:

Production build (vite build)passes, with a bundle-size warning

Open blockers called out most consistently across both documents:

  • The lead-generation forms are not fully turnkey: they depend on a manually configured VITE_MAIL_ENDPOINT and PHP backend, with English-only runtime/server messages.
  • Real business contact data is missing — src/data/contact.ts currently has no sales contacts filled in.
  • Dark/light theme work is present but not fully integrated or QA'd.
  • The visual design is still the earlier dark, brochure-style presentation; redesign-plan.md proposes moving to a lighter, denser, ecommerce-style catalog with Rafin navy/gold branding.
  • Some content is still placeholder or illustrative rather than production-ready.

10 Where to go from here

This document is meant to stay accurate as a baseline, not to be the final word. Three natural next conversations:

  1. Content completion — fill in real sales contacts, finish translating any remaining English strings, and grow the catalog toward the scale the taxonomy already supports.
  2. Visual direction — decide whether to execute the redesign plan as written, adjust it, or shelve it.
  3. Operational readiness — get the mail pipeline running against a real mailbox, fix the image audit script, and work through the launch checklist in PROJECT_COMPLETION_TODOS.md.
