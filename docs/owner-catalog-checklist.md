**Who this is for:** the business owner or sales lead at GENERAL TRADING.

actually have and sell. Anything shown on the site must be true on launch day.

## 1. Launch blockers (fix these first)

### 1.1 Photos that do not show the listed product

At least one photo shows a **different brand** from the listing:

> **Bomag BW213D Roller** (`/equipment/heavy-equipment/bomag-bw213d-roller`): the photo shows a
> **Caterpillar** roller, with the Caterpillar name visible on the machine. A buyer, or Bomag,
> could see this as misleading.

Every product photo on the site was picked as a **generic or "category-correct" stand-in**, not a
photo of your actual unit. The image records say so for each one ("generic", "category-correct
match", "stand-in", "may differ from the photograph"). For each product, choose one:

- [ ] **A. Replace** with a photo of your actual unit (best for trust and for Google Images), or
- [ ] **B. Keep for now** and accept a note such as "Illustrative photo" on the page, or
- [ ] **C. Hide the listing** until a real photo exists.

### 1.2 Photo licenses that need a credit on the page

**15 of the 20 product photos** are licensed CC BY or CC BY-SA (from Flickr and Wikimedia
Commons). These licenses **require a visible credit** (author, license, link). **The site does not
show any credits today.** Using them this way breaks the license terms.

- [ ] Replace them with your own photos (recommended, and this also fixes 1.1), **or**
- [ ] Approve adding a small credit line under each photo (we can build this), **or**
- [ ] Confirm you have separate written permission from each photographer.

Three photos (RIDGID 535, Fischer FIS EM Plus, Knauf Fireboard) are marked "internal reference
asset" with **no known source**:

- [ ] Tell us where they came from and confirm you may use them, or replace them.

### 1.3 Serial numbers and SKUs

All 20 serial numbers follow one pattern (`…RFM01` to `…RFM20`), and all SKUs start with `RAF-`.
They look like placeholders.

- [ ] Give the **real SKU / stock code** for each product (the code your team actually uses), **or**
      confirm that `RAF-` is your real code system.
- [ ] Give the **real serial number** for each used machine, **or** tell us to hide serial numbers
      (the page then shows "Confirmed during inquiry"). Never publish a serial you have not checked.

### 1.4 Product texts that admit they are placeholders

Some product pages contain wording that only makes sense for a demo. For example, the Albanian
text says "Njësi ilustrative" (illustrative unit) or "Paketë ilustrative" (illustrative package),
and the English text mentions the "frontend" or says "Visible price used as a frontend reference
only". Affected products:

- #2 Kubota U55-4, #9 Perkins 220 kVA, #14 Schneider Acti9 iC60N, #16 RIDGID 535,
  #17 Fischer FIS EM Plus, #18 Knauf Fireboard, #20 Caterpillar 1.2 m³ bucket
- [ ] Confirm these products are real. We will then rewrite the texts from your answers in section 2,
      or remove the products.
